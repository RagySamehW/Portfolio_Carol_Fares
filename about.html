    Ten years of making things that hold up.

    Carol Fares is a graphic designer working across brand identity, editorial layout, and packaging. The work starts with a question most clients haven't fully answered yet: what does this actually need to say, and to whom?

    Ten years in, the process hasn't changed much. Every project gets a real grid, a type system built to hold weight, and enough structure that the next person touching it doesn't have to guess. I work with clients across time zones, mostly over video calls and too much coffee.

    The short version

    2016

    Started out

    Junior designer at a small print studio, learning production constraints the hard way.

    2018

    Went independent

    Left to freelance full-time, picking up small identity and poster projects for local clients.

    2020

    First real system

    Built a full brand system for an independent publisher, the first project with genuine production demands.

    2022

    Added packaging

    Structural packaging joined the practice, one die line and one shelf test at a time.

    2025

    Now

    Mostly brand identity, editorial layout, and packaging, for clients who want a system, not just a logo.
