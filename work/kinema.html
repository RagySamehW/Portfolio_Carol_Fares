    Kinema Film Festival

    Client
    Kinema Film Festival
    Scope
    Identity, posters
    Year
    2025

    Kinema is a mid-size festival that had outgrown its old mark. The brief was a system that could hold a single feature poster and a full week of screening signage without losing its edge. The result is a mark built from a single cut frame, a type pairing sharp enough for signage, and a poster grid that still leaves room for the film.
